# Blocktickets design system

How blocktickets looks, sounds and moves, for anyone (or any agent) building UI in this repo.

**Sources of truth, in order**

1. `app/globals.css` holds the real tokens (`:root` is dark, `:root[data-nt="light"]` is light) and the `@theme inline` map that exposes them to Tailwind.
2. The brand guidelines site, `brand/index.html` (live at https://blocktickets-brand.vercel.app), holds the specs, copyable code and live demos. Page routes are hash links, for example `#/buttons`, `#/cards`, `#/motion`.
3. This file is the short version. If it disagrees with the guidelines site, the guidelines site wins, then fix this file.

Stack: Next.js 16, Tailwind CSS 4, shadcn/ui (`new-york`, `neutral`), Radix via the `radix-ui` package, Phosphor icons, Geist.
Read `node_modules/next/dist/docs/` before writing Next.js code, see `AGENTS.md`.

---

## 1. The brand in one minute

- **Idea:** Build bigger. Block by block. Organizations in control of their revenue, data and fans.
- **Look:** a neutral field, **one green**, large rounded cards, real sports photography, layered rounded sheets, frosted glass, seat dots.
- **Type:** Geist for text and headlines, Geist Mono for numbers, labels and captions.
- **Themes:** dark and light, same structure. Dark is the app default.
- **Voice:** short, direct, specific. Every number has a named source.
- **Budget:** about one green moment per viewport. Green is the accent, never the surface.

Rules

- Text on green is navy. Never white on green.
- Green on white fails contrast for small text. Use `--green-ink` for small green text.
- Red means a loss or a risk, in one place at a time. Never as decoration.
- A solid destructive button is white on `#C93A2E` in both themes (5.3:1).
- Colours outside the palette are not allowed. Gradients are not used, except a soft neutral scrim under text on photos.

## 4. Shape, spacing, layout

- **Radius gotcha:** in our theme `rounded-xl`, `rounded-2xl` and `rounded-3xl` are 24, 28 and 36px; in the reference they are 12, 16 and 24px. When porting a reference style, write the pixel value (`rounded-[24px]` for cards, alerts and surface accordions, `rounded-[12px]` for the close button) and never the named size.
- **Radius:** pill `999px` for actions and labels (buttons, badges, tabs, toggles, switches, pagination), **fields `12px`** (input, textarea, select, combobox, input group, OTP, number field), small tiles and icon tiles `8–12px`, attachments `12px`, dialogs `24px` (alert dialogs `16px`), cards `24px`. Always write explicit values (`rounded-[12px]`): in this theme `rounded-xl` is 24px, not 12px. Hero and closing cards on the guidelines site keep `28–36px`.
- **Spacing:** 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 32, 48, 64. Gaps between cards 12–16. Sections 64 apart. Inside a card 20 on phones, 28–44 on wide cards.
- **Container:** content width `1240px` (`1440px` from 1536px viewports), side padding 16px on phones and 32px from 640px. Use `container-x`.
- **Breakpoints:** 480, 640, 760 (nav pill appears), 960, 1180, 1536.
- **Surfaces:** paper → card → fill gives depth. Hairlines are `var(--line)`. Glass (nav, chips on photos) is white or surface at 12–60% with `backdrop-filter: blur(12–16px)`.

## 5. Elevation

Two kinds of card.

- **Flat:** `bg-card`, 24px radius, no border, no shadow. Use in grids and lists.
- **Raised:** `--surface` fill with `--sh-1`, lifting to `--sh-2` and `translateY(-2px)` on hover. Use for cards that can be pressed.

```css
--sh-1: 0 1px 2px rgb(10 10 10 / .05), 0 2px 8px -2px rgb(10 10 10 / .08);
--sh-2: 0 2px 4px rgb(10 10 10 / .04), 0 12px 28px -10px rgb(10 10 10 / .16);
--sh-3: 0 4px 8px rgb(10 10 10 / .05), 0 24px 56px -16px rgb(10 10 10 / .28);
/* dark theme: a 1px ring at 5–7% white plus a black shadow at .5–.65 */
```

`--sh-2` is for hover, popovers and menus. `--sh-3` is for dialogs and sheets. Shadows are soft, neutral and low. Never coloured on cards, never harder than `--sh-3`. The dark feature card carries a 1px inner ring: `inset 0 0 0 1px rgb(255 255 255 / .08)`.

## 6. Buttons

Always pills. Press scales to 97% (sm 98%, lg 96%) in 250ms and shows a ripple (HeroUI PressableFeedback: a circle in the text colour grows from the press point in 150ms at 12% opacity, held at least 225ms, then fades in 200ms; keyboard presses ripple from the centre; none under reduced motion, on disabled or pending buttons). Code: `lib/ripple.ts`, used by Button and CloseButton. One primary button per screen. Source: `components/ui/button.tsx`, Storybook `Actions/Button`.

**Hierarchy (one hue per row):** only two buttons carry colour, the green primary and the red danger. Everything else steps down through neutrals with ink text, so any mix of buttons in one line looks consistent.

| Level | Style | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | green fill, navy text (10.6:1) | The one main action on a screen |
| Secondary | neutral fill, ink text | Other real actions next to the primary, on the page ground. In dark it is a step lighter than cards and overlays, so it reads on them |
| Tertiary | soft neutral fill, ink text | Minor actions (Share, More), and the neutral button on a card, dialog, drawer or sheet |
| Ghost | no fill, ink text, fill on hover | Inline and toolbar actions |
| Outline | 1px border, ink text | Low-emphasis alternatives (Save draft) |
| Danger | red fill, white text | Destructive actions only |
| Danger soft | soft red fill, red text | Destructive actions that are not the main action |
| Navy, Navy green | brand navy fill (lightens on hover), white text (17.3:1) or green text (10.6:1) | **On request only**, never a default and never chosen by an agent unprompted. Strongest on light surfaces: navy is 1.1:1 against the dark page, so on dark it reads by its text |

Ink text is navy in the light theme and white in the dark theme. Blue is not used on buttons: it is the info colour (badges, alerts), and blue secondary buttons made mixed rows look inconsistent and read poorly on dark. Green is not used on secondary or tertiary buttons because green means success. **Variants (final set):** primary, secondary, tertiary, ghost, outline, danger, danger-soft, glass. The old names (`default`, `destructive`, `chip`, `inverse`, `link`, `navy`, `navy-green`) and sizes (`default`, `xs`, `icon-xs`) are gone: use the names above. Secondary and tertiary are different in dark too: secondary is the field grey, tertiary is a translucent tint of the text colour (7%, 12% on hover), so it stays visible on the page, a card and a dialog alike, instead of going near-black. `glass` is a translucent tint of the page text colour with a hairline ring and blur, so it reads on photos, white and dark; for inline text use the `Link` component.

**Colour states (as the reference):** hover and press mix the fill 90% with the button's text colour in the reference, and its text is white, so hover lightens. Our accent text is navy, which made the green darker, so the green and danger fills mix 10% white instead and lighten on hover. Pressed uses the hover fill. Neutral fills step 4% toward their text colour.

**Danger and status:** destructive actions start as `danger-soft`; the solid `danger` fill is for the confirm step only. A status (confirmed, paid) is a Badge with a check icon, never a button-like green fill. Story: `Actions/Button` > Danger and status pairing.

**Hold to confirm (needed for irreversible actions):** deleting, cancelling an order, paying, publishing, or any big release must use `HoldButton` (`components/ui/hold-button.tsx`, Storybook `Actions/Hold button`), not a plain click. It exists to prevent accidental clicks and to make the person take a breath before pushing: press and hold, an overlay sweeps across over 2 seconds (the reference `PressableFeedback.HoldConfirm`), release early and it snaps back in 200ms with nothing done, hold to the end and `onHoldComplete` fires. Works with mouse, touch and held Enter or Space. A plain confirm dialog can still follow for the very worst cases, but never rely on a quick single click for them. `onHoldProgress(0..1)` reports the hold every frame (filling and snapping back), so something else can follow it, such as the ticket that morphs into the recipient while you hold to send; Storybook story WithProgress. **Fill colours:** the primary hold fills with a deeper shade of its own green with white text (`color-mix` of `--h-accent` and black, 52%), so progress reads without a jump to another hue (it was navy before); the danger holds keep their reds. The fill reaches 1px past the button edge so no hairline of the button shows around it; Storybook story PrimaryFill.

**Error shake (inputs):** a field that turns invalid shakes (the "Error state shake" transition from transitions.dev: 6px right, 6px left, 4px overshoot, 280ms) and its `FieldError` message fades in over 280ms. It is pure CSS on `aria-invalid` / `data-invalid`, for input, textarea and input group; tokens are the `--shake-*` variables in `app/globals.css`. No motion under reduced motion.

**Skeleton (one style):** the transitions.dev pulse only: the block fades to 50% and back over 1s, ease-in-out, repeating while loading (`--pulse-*` in `app/globals.css`). There is no shimmer, no `animation` prop and no static variant.

**Skeleton reveal:** `SkeletonReveal` (`components/ui/skeleton.tsx`) swaps a skeleton for real content with the "Skeleton loader and reveal" transition from transitions.dev: both layers share one grid cell so nothing shifts, then cross-fade with a 2px cross-blur over 400ms (`--reveal-dur`, `--reveal-blur`, `--reveal-ease` in `app/globals.css`). Content is inert while loading. Use it wherever a skeleton gives way to data.

**Sizes:** sm 36px (32px from the `md` breakpoint), md 40px (36px), lg 48px (44px). 14px text at weight 500 (lg 16px). Icon-only buttons are circles at the same sizes and always carry an `aria-label`. Props: `fullWidth`, `pending` (spinner before the label, presses blocked), `iconOnly`. The `atoms/Button` wrapper takes `iconOnly` too, so carousel arrows are round, not oval. The old 44/48/56px sizes are gone; `size="sm"` used to be 44px, pass `lg` where the bigger size is wanted.

**Landing pages:** the atom wrapper `components/atoms/Button.tsx` (renders a link when given `href`) now accepts `size="lg"` as well as `md` and `sm`. Hero and closing calls to action use `lg`. On surfaces that are dark in both themes (hero photo, closing card) the second button is `glass`, with `--foreground` pinned on that surface so the glass stays white in the light theme too (glass follows `--foreground`, falling back to the theme text colour).

**Icons in buttons:** Phosphor Bold, 16px at every size (see Icon rules). Gap 8px, `-2px` side margin on text buttons. Labels are verb phrases. Disabled is 50% opacity.

**Icons do not move on hover.** An icon or arrow inside a button, and an icon-only button, stays where it is on hover, on every page. Hover feedback is the fill change; press feedback is the scale. Do not add a nudge, slide or per-icon hover transform to a button.

**Button group** (`Actions/Button group`; behaviour notes: a pressed button in a group keeps its size, as in the reference, so the group never breaks apart; hover changes only the button under the pointer, and a tertiary button's hover is a step lighter than its fill in dark, where the soft and default neutrals are the same colour; outline buttons share borders, so the touching edges drop theirs, horizontally and vertically; corners are logical, so the group mirrors in right-to-left; `isDisabled` is an alias of `disabled`): `variant`, `size` (sm 36/32, md 40/36, lg 48/44, phone/desktop), `disabled` and `fullWidth` on the group apply to every button through context; a button's own props win. Outer ends are pills, inner corners square, no gap, no press scale. A 1px separator (half height, 15% of the text colour) goes inside every item after the first; vertical groups get a horizontal one. Use separators between solid, secondary and ghost buttons; outline needs none. `ButtonGroupText` is a text cell with the same height and fill as a tertiary button.

## 7. Icons

Phosphor only (`@phosphor-icons/react`, `iconLibrary: "phosphor"`). No emoji, no hand-drawn icons.

- Regular for lists, nav, inputs. Bold inside buttons and at 16px or smaller. Fill for the active or selected state. Duotone for empty states and feature cards. Light and Thin only at 48px and up.
- Sizes: 14–20 in buttons, 16 labels and chips, 20 lists and nav, 24 cards, 32–48 empty states.
- One weight per screen. Icons take the ink colour. Green only on a green button or dot, navy on green.
- An icon never carries the only meaning. Decorative icons are `aria-hidden`.
- Icon in a shape: rounded square (30% radius) or circle, icon at 55% of the shape, one shape per set.

## 9. Motion

Soft, quick, purposeful. One idea animates at a time. Nothing loops forever, except loading indicators.

| Token | Value |
|---|---|
| `--ease` ease-out | `cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1)` |
| ease-land | `cubic-bezier(.34, 1.3, .64, 1)` |
| ease-press | `cubic-bezier(.65, 0, .35, 1)` |
| reveal | 500–700ms, fade + rise 16px + slight blur |
| stagger | 60–120ms between items |

| Interaction | Motion |
|---|---|
| Press | scale .97, 160ms |
| Tab indicator | slides and resizes, 280ms `cubic-bezier(.32, .72, 0, 1)` |
| Popover | opens from its trigger: 90% → 100% with a few px of travel and a small overshoot (`bt-pop-in`, 260ms), closes in 110ms |
| Tooltip | the same from 85% with a 4px blur-to-sharp (`bt-tip-in`, 200ms); no animation when one is already open |
| Menu | scale .95 → 1 and fade from the trigger, 180ms |
| Dialog | fade and scale 1.05 → 1 in 250ms, with a 4px offset for top or bottom placement; backdrop fades in 150ms |
| Switch | thumb stretches 6px while pressed |
| Checkbox | box fills, check draws in, 200ms |
| Accordion | height and opacity ease open, 200ms; caret turns 180 degrees in 250ms |
| Skeleton | soft sweep, 1.4s linear |
| Nav bar | blur and shrink after 8px, hide on scroll down, show on scroll up |

Numbers count up once, then hold, and the final value is in the HTML. Always respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: show the finished state, no shimmer, sweep or slide.

### Motion rules for components (from the motion review)
- **Tokens** (in `app/globals.css`): `--e-out: cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)` for enters and presses (the old reference curve was weaker), `--e-in-out: cubic-bezier(0.77, 0, 0.175, 1)` for on-screen movement, `--e-drawer: cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)` for panels; `--d-press` 120ms, `--d-fade` 150ms, `--d-overlay` 200ms. Components use the easing tokens; durations stay under 300ms.
- **Tooltips:** 500ms open delay; once one is open, the next opens instantly with no animation (`skipDelayDuration` 300ms).
- **Keyboard-opened surfaces do not animate.** `DialogContent instant` removes the enter and exit animation. The command palette now follows its own spec and animates (see Command below); pass `instant` to `CommandDialog` for a palette that is only ever opened with the keyboard.
- **Press feedback:** button, toggle, tabs trigger, switch scale to 0.97 (the select trigger does not scale: it holds text and would shift it), checkbox to 0.9, in 120–250ms. Accordion rows and menu items do not scale.
- **Reduced motion:** a global rule keeps overlay fades and removes zoom, slide and press-scale movement.
- **Touch targets:** small controls keep their look but get an invisible 44px hit area with `relative h-touch-44` (input clear and reveal buttons, input-group icon buttons, copy button, close button, label info button). Not for elements with `overflow-hidden`; checkbox, radio and switch rely on their label row (32px tall) instead.
- **Framer Motion (`framer-motion`) is used only where CSS cannot do it well:** a shared layout animation or an element that must animate out before it unmounts. Everything else stays CSS. Presets live in `lib/motion.ts` (`EASE_OUT`, `usePop`).
  - **Tabs:** the active pill glides between triggers (shared `layoutId`, spring with no bounce, 220ms). Variants: `soft` (the default, HeroUI's primary look: a neutral lighter pill on a quiet 4px track; `tone="accent"` tints the pill with the accent instead), `default` (the old filled bar with a green pill, now an option). Options: `separator` (a thin divider between tabs, hidden beside the selected one), `align` (start, center or end label alignment), vertical orientation for both the pill and the underline (the underline then runs down the side with the accent bar on the active tab), `outline` (every tab outlined, the active one has a stronger outline that glides) and `line` (a baseline under the row with a green 2px underline that glides, active label in the foreground colour). `size` sm / md / lg follows the shared control height ladder (sm 36 / 32, md 40 / 36, lg 48 / 44), `fullWidth` shares the row equally, `scrollable` lets a long row scroll sideways with soft fades at the edges, `orientation="vertical"` gives a side list. Tab labels can hold an icon or a small soft count badge; a tab with `iconOnly` holds just an icon and is a round pill (give it an `aria-label`). The previous line note: the `line` variant used a bare underline. The `soft` variant has no bar: the active tab sits in a neutral pill (the default fill) that glides between tabs. Tabs sit edge to edge with no gap, in every variant. An inactive tab shows a faint pill on hover: the default fill at 60% in `soft` and `line`, a foreground wash (8%) on the bar of the default variant, which keeps its green active pill and the glide. Reduced motion jumps instantly.
  - **Input adornments:** the clear button, the loading spinner and the valid check fade and scale in and out (0.85 to 1, 120ms) instead of popping. **Copy field:** the copy icon swaps to the check the same way.
  - Not animated on purpose: keyboard actions, anything triggered many times a day, and data (counts, progress values).
- **Transitions:** list exact properties, never `transition-all`. Sheet opens in 300ms and closes in 200ms on the drawer curve.

## 10. Voice and claims

- Plain, specific, confident. A partner, not a vendor. Never padded.
- Sentence case. Verb-phrase buttons. No exclamation marks, no shouting caps.
- Every number has a named partner, baseline and period. Partner names, logos, quotes and figures need written sign-off.
- No absolute claims ("zero fraud", "100%", "best") and no hype words.
- Photos are real and rights-cleared, under a dark gradient if text sits on them.

## 11. Accessibility

- Text contrast 4.5:1, large text 3:1. Check pairs against the guidelines (`#/accessibility`).
- Visible focus on everything interactive: 2px ink outline with 3px offset, or the 3px green ring on fields.
- Touch targets 44px or more. Hover is never the only way to reach something.
- Alt text on meaningful images, `aria-label` on icon-only controls, `aria-hidden` on decoration.

## 12. Do not

- Hardcode hex values, or add colours outside the palette.
- Use gradients, heavy or coloured shadows, or borders on flat cards.
- Use emoji or non-Phosphor icons, or Lucide.
- Set text below 13px, centre long text, or justify text.
- Add a second green moment or a second primary button.
- Animate things users repeat all day (keyboard actions, command menu open and close).

### Component styling and quality gates

Components are the shadcn components restyled: `cva` variants and Tailwind utilities on the `h-` tokens, merged with `cn()` (tailwind-merge), with `data-slot` and state data attributes as hooks. A caller's `className` overrides the component's own classes predictably, including at breakpoints.

**Decision record:** a move to per-component BEM class stylesheets (`styles/components/*.css`, with `@apply`) was tried for 40 components and reversed. It changed how a caller's `className` overrides a component (the stylesheet layer sits below utilities, so a plain `h-8` beat the component's own `md:h-9` and broke the calendar, carousel, sidebar and date-field presets), the class names had no type checking, and it split the codebase into two styles. Do not reintroduce per-component stylesheets.

**Quality gates** (all in `scripts/quality`, run locally and in CI):
- `npm run typecheck`, `npm run lint`, `npm run check-stories`: types, lint, every component has a story.
- `npm run visual`: loads every story in Chrome in dark and light and compares the computed style of every element (box, colours, spacing, type, shadows) with the baseline for the current platform (`tests/visual/baseline/darwin` on macOS and `tests/visual/baseline/linux` on Linux). Any unintended visual change fails and names the element and property. Use `-- --filter <regex>` to check some stories, `-- --static` to run against a built Storybook, or `-- --platform <darwin|linux>` to select a baseline folder. The override only selects the files and Chrome still runs on the host platform.
- After reviewing an intended visual change in Storybook, update **both** baselines. On macOS, run `npm run visual:update` locally to refresh `tests/visual/baseline/darwin`. For Linux, run **Generate Linux visual baseline** on GitHub Actions for the revision you are reviewing, then download `visual-baseline-linux` and replace the contents of `tests/visual/baseline/linux` with the artifact. Review both sets of changes before committing.
- A caller that passes sizing or text classes to a component should be checked by this too; it does not see closed popovers or hover states, so test those in Storybook.

### Fields (input, textarea, select, number field)
- **Fills (variants):**
  - `primary`: a flat fill a clear step off the page with a crisp 1px inner edge and no shadow, the same recipe in both themes. Light is a faint grey (`oklch(0.977 0.002 286)`) with a 7% dark edge; dark is a lifted dark grey (`oklch(0.238 0.006 286)`, the surface is 0.2103) with a 7% white edge. On focus the ring replaces the edge and the light fill turns white. The light `secondary` variant has no edge and a lighter fill than primary (`oklch(0.997 0 286)`, hover `0.988`).
  - `secondary`: for fields on a card or surface. A lighter fill than primary with no edge (`--h-field-secondary`: near white `oklch(0.997 0 286)` in light, the field fill with 7% white mixed in for dark, so the field stands apart from the card behind it). Hover and focus stay on the same fill, focus adds the ring. Available on input, textarea, native select and number field.
  - `tertiary`: no fill, a 1px outline (`--h-border-secondary`, darker on hover), for forms on top of cards and for low-emphasis fields.
  - `underlined`: no fill or box, only a 1px bottom line (2px accent on focus, 2px red when invalid), no radius, no side padding. For dense forms, tables and inline editing.
  - `bordered`: a visible 1px border (20% of the text colour) on the surface fill. Hover strengthens the border to 28% and takes the secondary surface; focus is an accent border with a 2px ring at 20%; invalid is a danger border, a soft danger fill and danger text. For forms on plain pages that want a defined edge.
  - `shape="pill"` makes any variant fully round (search boxes).
- **One neutral fill in dark:** every neutral fill (`--h-default`, `--h-default-soft` and their hovers) is the field fill, `oklch(0.238 0.006 286)`. Inputs, button groups, secondary and tertiary buttons, switch and slider tracks, kbd, toggles and the AM/PM toggle all share it. It is set once in the dark override block of `app/globals.css`; change it there. Surfaces and overlays (cards, surface tags, table cells, menus, popovers, dialogs) stay on their own darker grey (`--h-surface` and `--h-overlay`, `oklch(0.2103 0.0059 286)`), so a primary field or neutral button on a card reads as a slightly lifted box; the `secondary` field variant sits a step lighter again. The one step up is `surface-secondary`, the container behind a table or a grouped area. Coloured fills (green, red, blue, purple, amber) are untouched. Exceptions: alerts and the surface accordion use the field fill in dark instead of the surface colour, like inputs and the calendar; switch and slider tracks use `--h-track` (`oklch(0.33 0.006 286)` in dark, the default fill in light) so they stay visible against the page. A standalone calendar sits on a rounded field-fill panel; inside a popover or card it is transparent. Switch thumbs are white and radio dots a dark green in both themes.

- **Glass menus:** every floating menu or popover (dropdown and context menu, menubar, select, inline select, popover, hover card, autocomplete, colour picker, command palette, chart tooltip, floating action bar) has the `glass` class (`app/globals.css`), a recipe taken from heroui.pro: translucent fill (`--glass-fill`: white at 66% in light, the overlay grey at 60% in dark), `blur(40px) saturate(1.5)`, a thick (2px), faint top reflection (`--glass-highlight`: white at 20% in light, 6% in dark) and a faint 1px rim (`--glass-edge`), both inset shadows. The nav uses the same recipe scroll-linked: fill, blur and the hairline and rim fade in with `--bg` as you scroll, and the island, chapter row, search panel and suggestion list use it at full strength. Dialogs, sheets, drawers, tooltips and toasts stay opaque. **Fills by role:** menus and popovers `--glass-fill` (white 78% light, overlay grey 72% dark, the most opaque because they hold text), panels `--glass-fill-panel` via `glass-panel` (70% / 64%: command palette, search panel), floating bars and small tips `--glass-fill-float` via `glass-float` (58% / 52%: action bar, chart tooltip). The nav bar sits at about 60 to 70% scroll-linked.
